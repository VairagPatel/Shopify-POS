01 / START HERE

We are replacing the till interface.

The cashier currently uses Shopify POS. The proposed replacement is our Eluno till app. Shopify remains the backend where sales and refunds must be visible for finance and the website.

One important correction: we do not fetch data from the Shopify POS screen. Our backend talks to Shopify-style APIs. In this project those APIs are supplied mocks. Prescriptions and staff come from a separate store-tool service; payments come from a separate terminal service.
INPUT

What already exists

Customer, tags, products, prices, stock, stores, staff and prescriptions.

OUR WORK

What we add

Role checks, family carts, clinical validation, offer decisions, GST calculation, invoice sequence and safe transaction orchestration.

OUTPUT

What gets recorded

A paid source order, store invoice, payment reference, stock change, and source-backed reporting. Returns create linked refunds and credit-note evidence.

“I built a store billing layer that reads the existing business data, validates and prices a sale, then coordinates payment and the source order while tracking failures durably.”

Use this as your opening explanation. Then demonstrate one bill from input to source evidence.

02 / ARCHITECTURE & OWNERSHIP

Two interfaces. One business process.

The frontend and Swagger both call the same FastAPI backend. Swagger exposes requests and schemas; the frontend makes the workflow easier for store staff. Neither independently calculates the authoritative bill.

The entire sequence in one read
  1. Authenticate. The browser receives a session cookie. The backend checks role, store ownership and a CSRF token on writes. Staff identity in the request must match the signed-in staff account, except the documented admin behavior.
  2. Read source data. Load products and location, find the customer, fetch staff and wearer prescriptions, and read inventory for HSR. No money or stock moves.
  3. Build the cart. Associate selections with wearers. Multiple frames and repeated SKU units are supported through members. Frames and lenses pair in their selection order within each wearer; extra items remain standalone.
  4. Prepare. Backend fetches source values, checks stock demand and Rx compatibility, evaluates offers, calculates inclusive GST, reserves a store invoice number and saves the immutable quote. The status is draft. There is no terminal charge, source order or stock reservation yet.
  5. Confirm payment. Backend loads the saved quote and rechecks stock. It persists a payment reference and pending state before sending a single UPI/card charge request. Cash requires an open drawer and bypasses the electronic terminal.
  6. Create the source order. After a successful charge, add the payment transaction to the prepared payload and POST the order. The source decrements tracked inventory. Save the returned order ID and mark order_recorded; mark the invoice issued.
  7. Read back and reconcile. Fetch checkout detail, which includes the actual source order. Display invoice and references. Source reports include the sale in its store/channel and compare recorded source payments with sales/refunds.
A quote is not a completed sale. A displayed amount only proves calculation. A charge ID proves a payment response. A source order ID plus the fetched order proves the sale was written back. Demonstrate all three stages separately.

What goes back to the source

Variant IDs and quantities • Original inclusive prices • Per-line discount allocations • Tax lines • Customer and location • Wearer/Rx properties • Store invoice, staff and channel notes • Successful payment transaction and charge reference.

If HSR starts with 3 units of this frame: reading data leaves 3, preparing the quote leaves 3, successful order creation leaves 2, and a successful full refund restocks it to 3 at HSR. The made-to-order lens is not returned to sellable stock.

Offer policy: the implemented rules select eligible stackable offers versus a non-stackable option according to the configured policy. The requested “festive + second pair + priority, always together” is not automatically guaranteed. The stakeholder conflict still needs agreement.

05 / THE OTHER JOURNEYS

The sale is the starting point.

Family / multiple items

One payer, several wearers

Add up to ten wearer entries, each with up to thirty units. Every lens uses its own wearer's valid prescription. Repeated variant IDs represent units; the source payload currently keeps a line per unit.

Check: one order/invoice, correct Rx references on lens lines, total demand across all wearers within stock. A second standalone frame must not replace the first or become a complete pair for offers.

Clinical

Stop an incompatible lens

The backend checks prescription expiry and lens metadata limits. New clinical axis entries use five-degree steps. A rejected quote must not charge money.

Check: out-of-range sphere/cylinder, missing progressive ADD, expired Rx, invalid axis and unauthorized clinician writes.

Full return

Reverse both money and the sale

Manager prepares a full refund from the original source order. The source calculates refundable tax/amount; our app verifies them. Confirmation refunds the terminal (or handles cash), then writes the source refund.

Output: linked refund evidence and credit note referencing the original invoice; frame restocked to billing location, lens not restocked. Partial line refunds are not implemented.

Remake

Replace the lens, retain the frame

Manager identifies the original sale, wearer, new lens, reason, lab cost and cost owner. A new lens-only checkout is created. Company/external-optometrist cost allocation makes the customer charge zero; customer-funded work can require payment.

Check: original invoice link and cost allocation retained; no new frame line or frame stock movement. This records the external cost owner; it does not collect that cost from the optometrist.

Cash drawer

Explain the closing balance

Open float + cash sales − cash refunds − recorded expenses = expected cash. Counted cash is compared with expected cash. An absolute variance strictly above ₹200 queues a finance alert.

Example: ₹2,000 + ₹9,000 − ₹0 − ₹300 = ₹10,700 expected. Count ₹10,450: variance −₹250, so queue an alert. Queued is not the same as emailed.

Reporting

Read the result from the source

Daily reports show bills, average bill value, discounts, store/channel and reconciliation data. The scheduler queues yesterday's founder digest after 10am IST, with a durable daily key to avoid duplicate queue entries.

Check: source sale/refund totals and IST dates; actual delivery needs SMTP configuration. SMTP uncertainty remains visible for investigation.

06 / FAILURE IS PART OF THE FLOW

A timeout does not tell us whether money moved.

SQLite stores the operation state before unsafe external writes. This lets the app distinguish “not started” from “sent, outcome uncertain.” The current mock terminal does not provide sufficient guarantees for blindly repeating a charge.

What happened?App behaviorWhat to tell the reviewer
Insufficient stock / incompatible RxReject before payment. Stale stock is rechecked at completion.“No charge was attempted; adjust the bill.”
Same completed checkout submitted againReturn the already-recorded result.“Same checkout and same source order, without another charge.”
Terminal received the charge but its reply was lostpayment_uncertain; block another completion attempt.“Investigate terminal outcome. Unknown does not mean unpaid.”
Charge succeeded; source order reply was lostorder_uncertain; manager reconciliation searches source orders by checkout reference.“Link an existing matching order; do not automatically POST another.”
No matching source order can be foundRemain unresolved for operational investigation.“We have not implemented guaranteed automatic completion or compensation.”
Refund reply lostExplicit terminal/source uncertain states. Source refunds may be reconciled by their saved reference.“Do not issue the money again just because the response was lost.”
Wi-Fi drops while editingDraft saved locally first; server draft attempted. Recovery depends on stage and saved state.“Draft persistence exists; seamless offline checkout is not proven.”

Safe GET requests have bounded retries for supported transient failures. Financial writes are sent once. The app's mutation lock protects its documented single-worker process; it is not a distributed lock or a stock reservation against other selling channels.

07 / ROLES & CONTROL

Permissions are enforced by the API.

RolePrimary workImportant limit
CashierPrepare/complete/cancel bills, save drafts, open drawer, record expenses, read own-store reports.No manager discount, refund, remake, clinical write or drawer close.
ManagerCashier actions + reasoned discounts up to 10%, refunds, remakes, reconciliation and drawer close.Own-store scope; cannot author prescriptions.
OptometristRead records and author validated prescriptions.No sales/refunds or money-changing actions.
FinanceCross-store reporting, audit, outbox and founder digest.No cashier sales, refunds or clinical writes.
AdminAll configured actions and cross-store access.Business validation still applies; not a bypass for invalid stock or prescriptions.

Why you see HSR even though seven stores exist

The seed contains Mantri Mall (MM), HSR Layout (HSR), Bel Road (BR), Koramangala (KMG), Jayanagar (JN), Indiranagar (IN), and Vega City (VC), plus HQ & Lab as a warehouse. All seven stores have sample orders; they are not seven copies of HSR.

The provisioned cashier, manager and optometrist accounts are assigned to HSR. Their store selector is restricted by the backend. Sign in as admin or finance to inspect all seven stores, inventory and reports. The report date matters: no bills on a chosen date does not mean a store has no data.

Most successful sale regression scenarios use HSR. Multi-store seed availability and store-access tests do not prove every operational scenario has been executed in all seven stores. Cashier/manager accounts for other stores must be provisioned with matching staff identities. A central admin changing stores must still select a valid source staff identity for that billing store.

The customer/catalog directory is shared. Store-scoped transaction, inventory and reporting requests are checked against the user's store. Knowing another store's checkout ID does not grant access.

Retail vs finance conflict: the manager can approve a discount directly within the limit, but must supply a reason; approver identity is retained. This addresses the no-approval-chain request without discarding audit evidence.
